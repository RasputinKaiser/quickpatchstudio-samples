# Vertical Ad Storyboard Sample

## 0-3 Seconds

Open with a single tension line: "Most puzzle games ask you to match. This one asks you to think one move deeper."

## 3-10 Seconds

Show the core mechanic in motion with fast captions:

- "Drag"
- "Combine"
- "Set up the chain"
- "Watch the payoff"

## 10-20 Seconds

Increase pace with two failed moves and one satisfying solve. Keep the game footage full-screen, with captions locked to the bottom safe area.

## 20-28 Seconds

CTA:

"Try the first levels free. Beat your cleanest solution."

## Deliverables

- 15-30 second vertical edit
- 2-3 alternate opening hooks
- Caption text and export notes
